The Pale Dawn is a prehistoric survival and management simulation game, currently a work in progress. It follows a band of nomadic hominids in the Late Pleistocene as they forage, hunt, make camp, and cross paths with other groups, such as Neanderthals, Denisovans, and the last of Homo erectus. Rather than a traditional strategy game, it is meant to be a "story generator": a simulation where the lost tales of humanity's longest era play out on their own.
